Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try ShiftEase before purchasing?
Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. You can also schedule a personalized demo with our team to see ShiftEase in action.
How many employees can I manage?
ShiftEase scales with your business. Our plans support teams of all sizes — from 5 employees to 5,000+. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time as your team grows.
Do employees need to download an app?
Employees can access ShiftEase via our mobile apps (iOS & Android) or through any web browser. The mobile app is free for all team members and includes shift swaps, time-off requests, and clock-in/out.
What integrations do you offer?
ShiftEase integrates with major payroll providers (ADP, Gusto, Paychex), POS systems, Slack, Google Calendar, and more. We also offer a robust REST API for custom integrations.
