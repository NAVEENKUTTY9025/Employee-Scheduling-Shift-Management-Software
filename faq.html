All Questions

What is ShiftEase and how does it work?
ShiftEase is a cloud-based employee scheduling and shift management platform. It allows managers to build schedules using drag-and-drop, track time and attendance, and ensure labor law compliance. Employees can view their schedules and swap shifts via our mobile app.
Can I try ShiftEase before purchasing?
Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. You can also schedule a personalized demo with our team to see ShiftEase in action.
How many employees can I manage?
ShiftEase scales with your business. Our plans support teams of all sizes — from 5 employees to 5,000+. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time as your team grows.
Do employees need to download an app?
Employees can access ShiftEase via our mobile apps (iOS & Android) or through any web browser. The mobile app is free for all team members and includes shift swaps, time-off requests, and clock-in/out.
What integrations do you offer?
ShiftEase integrates with major payroll providers (ADP, Gusto, Paychex), POS systems, Slack, Google Calendar, and more. We also offer a robust REST API for custom integrations.
Is my data secure with ShiftEase?
Yes. We take security seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard protocols (SSL/TLS). We are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant.
Can I import my existing schedule from Excel?
Yes, we provide a simple CSV template that you can fill out and upload directly into ShiftEase. Our onboarding team can also assist you with bulk importing your employee data and historical schedules.
How does the shift swap feature work?
Employees can request a shift swap directly from their mobile app. The request goes to the manager for approval. Once approved, the schedule is automatically updated for both employees, and compliance rules are checked instantly.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or startups?
Yes! We offer a 20% discount for registered non-profit organizations and early-stage startups (under 2 years old and less than 10 employees). Please contact our sales team for more details.
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel at any time. If you cancel, you will retain access to your account until the end of your current billing cycle. We can also provide a full export of your data (CSV) upon request.
